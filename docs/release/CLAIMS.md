## Summary

Implemented and reachable through `allo.compile`: the three languages; the
matcher with store coverage, accumulator-init facts, axis roles, exact-cast,
single-rounding float narrowing, resolved access coordinates with the
identity output-map contract, and output-copy contracts; the reduction-order policy; the target-neutral
legality pass; the movement stage with the Section IV algebra and searches
(ties realized on Samsung, UPMEM and AiM); lazy materialization with recorded
rejections and bounded joint evaluation; the cost evaluator; four matcher
backends plus the typed and source Gemini-II routes and the analytical AttAcc
backend. Partial: row 16 on APU v1 (work-grid owner map only). External
evaluation-only paths, documented as such: E11 (Gemini-I source-order run
script), the DRAM half of E14, E15, and the AttAcc reference-best study,
Ramulator2-AttAcc timing and DCC decode composition of E16.

Required by gate G9 (user scope addition 2026-11-02, ISSUES X-01) and in
progress: one shared orchestration with explicit stage contracts for every
target. Since round 12 every ordinary source workload passes the same front
stages (`allo/spmw_pipeline.py`: frontend, function-boundary check, matcher,
coverage/semantic contracts C-19 to C-27, shared facts
`allo/spmw_semantics.py`), and since round 13 the same candidate pipeline
(`compile_for_target`: enumeration, legality, movement stage, cost, ranking,
materialization), with the APU v1 vector plans and the Gemini-II launch plans
as candidates of `rank_matcher_placements` (rows 24 and E9/E10 now go through
it), since round 14 with supplied placements rebound to the current program
(C-28, C-29) and since round 15 under a field-by-field enforced candidate
contract (C-30) with the Gemini-II typed records as frontends of the same
pipeline, and since round 16 with `APUv1Program` as a frontend of the same
pipeline and the launch chain, accumulator modes and every operation's
extent enforced by the contract (C-30 follow-up). Since rounds 19-24 every
matcher backend (Samsung, AiM, UPMEM, AttAcc) declares `CandidateEffects`
from the shared facts and is checked against them at materialization; since
round 23 the host program's transfer availability is a shared stage; since
round 28 the `APUv1Program` manifest is read from the retained IR; round 29
traced forced alternatives and cost perturbations to generated source and
launches on both hardware hosts from installed wheels. Remaining for G9: the
independent review of that evidence and the installed-wheel simulator and
full-suite runs at one revision (`design/shared-compilation-path.md`).
